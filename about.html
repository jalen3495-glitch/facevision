About FaceVision

FaceVision is a free, privacy-first face detection tool that runs entirely in your web browser.

Detection, not recognition

FaceVision answers one question: where are the faces in this picture? It draws a box around each face and marks a few key points such as the eyes, nose and mouth. It does not work out who anyone is, compare faces against a database, or create face "fingerprints".

How it works

  1. Your browser loads Google's open-source MediaPipe library and a small BlazeFace detection model.
  2. You choose a photo, drop one onto the preview, or start your camera after granting permission.
  3. The model runs on your device (using your GPU when available, otherwise your CPU) and returns face positions and confidence scores.
  4. FaceVision draws the results on top of your image or video.

Privacy first

Your images and camera frames are processed locally and never uploaded. Usage analytics are off unless you switch them on, and even then only coarse, anonymous events are sent. Read the full privacy policy.
