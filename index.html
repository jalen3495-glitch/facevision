Privacy

Images and camera frames are processed in the browser and are not sent to the FaceVision API. MediaPipe's own runtime may send usage or performance metrics to Google.

Optional analytics

When enabled, and only if an analytics server is configured, we send product events and coarse face-count buckets. No images, landmarks, names or biometric templates are collected.

How it works

Three steps, all in your browser. Nothing to install.

1

Open a photo or camera

Choose an image, drop one onto the preview, or allow the camera after a clear permission prompt.

2

Detect on your device

A small BlazeFace model runs locally, using your GPU when it can, and falls back to the CPU if needed.

3

See boxes, not identities

FaceVision draws a box and a few keypoints. It does not name anyone or search a database.

Built for private, everyday use

A simple tool with clear limits — useful for photos, demos and learning how on-device detection works.

Your image stays here

Photos and camera frames are not uploaded to FaceVision. Close the tab and the preview is gone.

Detection, not recognition

Finding where a face is in a picture is not the same as identifying a person. We only do the first.

Works on phones

The layout is mobile-first. On a phone, use the front camera or pick a photo from your library.
