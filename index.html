Privacy

Images and camera frames are processed in the browser and are not sent to the FaceVision API. MediaPipe's own runtime may send usage/performance metrics to Google.

Optional analytics

When enabled, only product events and coarse face-count buckets are sent to the FaceVision API. No images, landmarks, names, or biometric templates are collected.
