Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what data FaceVision ("we", "us"), operated by Kiana Cupit, collects and why. The site is currently published at https://jalen3495-glitch.github.io/facevision/. FaceVision is a face detection tool: it finds where faces appear in an image. It does not identify people, compare faces, or build face templates.

1. Images and camera video stay on your device

When you choose an image or start your camera, the pictures are processed inside your browser using Google's MediaPipe Face Detector (the BlazeFace short-range model). Images and camera frames are never uploaded to us or to our optional analytics server. The camera only turns on after you confirm an in-page prompt, click "Allow camera", and your browser asks for permission. You can stop the camera or revoke permission at any time in your browser settings. We do not store your images, face coordinates, landmarks, or any biometric templates.

2. Third-party code loaded to run the detector

To run detection, your browser downloads:

  • the MediaPipe Tasks Vision library and its WebAssembly files from the jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net), and
  • the face detection model file from Google (storage.googleapis.com).

Like any web request, these providers receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser user agent. According to MediaPipe's own package notice, input data is processed on-device, but MediaPipe APIs may send performance and usage metrics to Google. These metrics are governed by Google's policies, not ours.

3. Optional anonymous analytics (off by default)

Analytics is opt-in. Nothing is sent unless you tick "Allow anonymous usage analytics" and the site owner has configured an analytics server. If enabled, each event contains only:

  • Event name — one of: detector_ready, detector_error, image_analyzed, camera_started, camera_stopped, camera_error, analytics_consent_granted, analytics_consent_denied;
  • Session ID — a random identifier stored in your browser's sessionStorage, which is cleared when you close the tab;
  • Page path (for example /index.html);
  • Properties, limited to: mode ("image" or "camera"), a coarse face-count bucket ("0", "1", "2-3" or "4+"), and an error category (for example "initialization");
  • Timestamp.

Your analytics choice is saved in your browser's localStorage so we remember it. The server does not store IP addresses. It briefly holds connection addresses in memory only to rate-limit abuse; these are never written to the database. Untick the box at any time to stop analytics.

4. Advertising and cookies

The site is set up so that display ads can be shown once a publisher account is approved. Until those codes are added, no advertising scripts are loaded and the ad slots stay empty. When ads are enabled, they are reserved banner or similar in-page units only. Pop-under and redirect ads are not used by default.

Advertising partners — which may include networks such as Adsterra, Monetag, HilltopAds, or, on a custom domain, Google AdSense — may set cookies or receive standard request information (such as IP address and user agent) in order to show ads. Those vendors process data under their own privacy policies. Ads are never placed over the camera view, and advertising scripts do not receive your images or camera video, which never leave your device.

Opting out: you can block or delete cookies in your browser, use a content blocker, or, for Google ads if they are later enabled, visit Google Ads Settings and youronlinechoices.eu.

EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: where required by law, we will ask for your consent before advertising cookies are used for personalised ads. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads, or no ads, depending on the network.

5. Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy statement.

6. Children

FaceVision is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect names, emails, images or IP addresses through the app, we usually cannot link analytics events to you. You can still contact us with any request about your data, and you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA.

8. Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when our practices change.
